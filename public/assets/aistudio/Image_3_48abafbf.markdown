## Brand & Style

This design system targets cinephiles, avid streamers, and culture connoisseurs seeking an immersive entertainment curation experience. The visual language evokes the ritualistic mystery of stepping into an upscale midnight movie premiere—illuminated by the luminescence of projection beams, neon marquees, and rich velvety darkness.

The aesthetic fuses **Glassmorphism** with **Atmospheric Dark Cinema**. Deep obsidian-navy structures serve as a light-absorbing canvas, allowing volumetric light flares, soft neon lilac diffusions, and playful hot pink halos to evoke the magic of the silver screen. Visual details reference film grain, reel counter marks, subtle clapboard slate striping, and anamorphic lens glows without falling into vintage pastiche. The emotional outcome is high-prestige, deeply immersive, and electric.

## Colors

The color architecture is built for an uncompromising dark mode experience that channels the illumination dynamics of cinema theater lighting:

- **Deep Navy Structural Base (`#0B0F19`, `#111827`, `#1E293B`)**: Serves as the primary stage. `#0B0F19` forms the infinite void background, while `#111827` and `#1E293B` shape floating cards, rails, and translucent frosted layers.
- **Electric Lilac / Soft Violet (`#A855F7`, `#8B5CF6`)**: The primary brand spectrum. Applied to marquee badges, active playback states, glowing category pills, and ambient atmospheric highlights.
- **Playful Neon Pink (`#F43F5E`, `#EC4899`)**: The secondary accent. Deployed for discovery markers, premiere highlights, heart/favorite states, and chromatic edge luminescence.
- **Warm Kinetic Orange (`#F97316`, `#FB923C`)**: The high-impact conversion tertiary. Exclusively reserved for primary conversion triggers (e.g., "Start Watching", "Watch Trailer", live event countdowns), cutting decisively through ambient purple/pink backdrops.
- **Text & Surface Luminance**: High-contrast off-whites (`#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`) ensure legibility against deep navy layers, while muted slate shades (`#94A3B8`, `#64748B`) anchor secondary film metadata (runtimes, audio channels, aspect ratios).

## Typography

The typographical pairing sets up a theatrical tension between old-world cinematic majesty and modern digital engineering:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines)**: Brings dramatic high-contrast serifs reminiscent of vintage film title sequences, classic cinema playbills, and prestigious film festival billing.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body Content)**: Offers refined, warm, geometric legibility that stays crisp over dark glass panels and vibrant backsplashes.
- **Space Grotesk (Labels, Badges & Metadata)**: Infuses a technical film-slate quality for timecodes, 4K HDR badges, Dolby Vision tags, critic ratings, and reel chapter labels. Set with deliberate positive letter-spacing and uppercase styling for precise technical gravitas.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that gracefully consolidates into 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with a 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter and generous 48px (`3rem`) outer margins. Content carousels break free from standard container bounds using bleed margins to replicate widescreen cinematic letterboxing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Media cards collapse from 4-across to 2-across with horizontal thumb-scroll trays.
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge buffers. Feature hero banners swap horizontal aspect ratios (21:9 or 16:9) to vertical key-art framing (9:16 poster cards).
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing strictly adheres to an 8pt base grid. Media carousels and genre clusters use `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) gaps between sections to preserve theatrical breathing room and avoid visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors the atmosphere of a dark cinema theater lit by dynamic projection cones:

- **Atmospheric Glow Foundations**: Deepest layers feature muted radial gradients (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.12)` and `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.08)`) with blur radiuses exceeding 120px, framing media content with subtle neon back-lighting.
- **Surface Level 1 (Background Deck)**: Pure solid `#0B0F19` with a subtle 2% noise texture to mimic organic 35mm film grain.
- **Surface Level 2 (Curated Shelves & Glass Paneling)**: `#111827` at 65% opacity combined with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a top-edge highlight (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Hovering Cards & Overlays)**: `#1E293B` at 80% opacity backed by a layered drop shadow:
  - Ambient base: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`
  - Edge neon emission: `0 0 25px -5px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)`
- **Interactive Focus States**: Trigger an elevated halo (`0 0 35px 2px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.4)` or hot pink equivalent) simulating direct marquee projection light.

## Shapes

The design uses **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature with specialized adaptations for cinema motifs:

- Standard interactive controls (inputs, cards, containers) utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radii, providing a modern, sleek geometry that balances the sharp serifs of display headlines.
- Large floating modals, spotlight trailers, and premier hero frames use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to preserve a soft screen-portal appearance.
- Pills, streaming quality chips (4K HDR), and clapboard status tags utilize full circular pill curves (`9999px`) to create high contrast against boxy video thumbnails and widescreen frames.
- Film reel counters and timecode stamps feature strict 4px micro-radii to maintain technical authenticity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic CTA ("Watch Now", "Start Trial")**: Solid energetic warm orange (`#F97316`) background with pure white typography. Features an inner top bevel highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) and an ambient glow shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.4)`). Scales by `1.02` with amplified glow on hover.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Button ("Add to Watchlist", "Details")**: Translucent glass (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`) with a 1px border tinted in soft lilac (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.4)`). Hover triggers a hot pink rim glow (`#F43F5E`).
- **Icon / Player Controls**: Circular floating glass discs with a blur of 12px, housing clean SVG glyphs in `#F8FAFC`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Format Chips (4K, HDR, Dolby Atmos)**: Pill-shaped, micro-sized `label-sm` Space Grotesk typography. Styled with dark navy fill (`#111827`), a subtle 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and neon lilac glow accents on key achievements.
- **Genre / Mood Selectors**: Interactive pill buttons. Inactive states feature `#1E293B` at 50% opacity. Active states ignite with a vivid gradient from `#A855F7` to `#F43F5E` and crisp white text.

### Movie & Media Cards
- **Poster Aspect (2:3) & Widescreen Aspect (16:9)**: Enclosed with `rounded-lg` borders and an invisible inset stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Hover Reveal**: On hover, poster scales smoothly (`scale(1.04)`), revealing a progressive bottom-up obsidian gradient (`linear-gradient(to top, #0B0F19 80%, transparent)`), a compact trailer preview button, match scores, and audio/video badges.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Theater Console**: Floating pill or rounded-lg search input wrapped in frosted obsidian glass (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.7)`). On focus, expands subtly with an outer neon lilac glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #A855F7, 0 0 20px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35)`). Placeholder text in muted slate (`#64748B`).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Checkboxes & Toggles**: Custom styled with deep navy tracks (`#111827`). When checked/active, surfaces illuminate in radiant orange (`#F97316`) or neon lilac (`#A855F7`) with matching soft bloom drop shadows.

### Unique Cinema Domain Elements
- **Curated Reel Scroller**: Custom scrollbars disguised as film perforation tracks.
- **Clapboard Chapter Indicator**: Diagonal micro-striped accents (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` alternating stripes) applied to episode dividers and premiere announcement ribbons.
- **Spotlight Beam Backdrop**: Top-center radial beam cone that sweeps across featured premiere titles upon page landing.
